Before your visit

Questions are welcome.

Is this a real dental clinic?

No. Vela is a fictional portfolio concept. No appointment or personal information is transmitted.

How would insurance support work?

A real implementation could explain accepted plans, estimates and claim support without guaranteeing coverage.

Will I see costs before treatment?

Yes in the fictional service model: options and indicative costs would be provided in writing before treatment begins.

What if I have an urgent dental concern?

This portfolio demo cannot assess symptoms. A production clinic site should make its urgent contact route and emergency limitations immediately clear.

Can I request accessibility adjustments?

The production flow would invite access requests before a visit and confirm them directly with the practice.
